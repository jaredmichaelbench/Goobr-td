# Enemies for the five-night demo (T-037, locked D-0061)

Claude Doc (live version): https://claude.ai/artifact/4yqjTfcXKg1sNTKeHT3kbN. Locked 2026-11-01 (Jared: "Let's rock with this").

## Ground rules
- Every attack is telegraphed and lands on the beat; parry on landing (D-0024).
- Each enemy asks one clear question; no two ask the same.
- Elites are told apart by weapon and shield, not tint (D-0060).
- Clutter is solved by the look, never by weaker enemies.
- Note targeting (Jared): while a boss or elite is in view, notes target it; otherwise the nearest creep.

## Names (code id: new name)
grunt: Bonehead · runner: Legger · swarm: Knucklebones · brute: Big Lad · shade: Hush · jammer: Spanner · archer: Pot-shot · warden: Crossing Guard · field boss: The Night Manager. Renamable any time.

## Six new enemies
| Name | Night | What it does | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Door-to-Door | 2 | Carries a door; hits from ahead are blocked, side/back land | Did you build for flank hits? | Maze turns, notes, Lightning |
| Last Orders (elite) | 3 | Rings a bell (1-bar telegraph); missed parry mutes the next bar's notes | Parry something not thrown at you | Parry the ring, kill early |

| Digger | 4 | Digs through the nearest wall in 4 bars; path reroutes | Guard your walls | Kill inside 4 bars, Thorny Walls, Cannon |
| Undertaker (elite) | 4 | Grave marks behind front towers; 3 Boneheads climb out 2 bars later | Parry a raise / towers deep in the maze | Parry the raise (beat 1), spread towers |
| Moths | 4 | Flock of 6-10, very low health, fly straight at the camp fire | What guards the camp? | Arrows near camp, Cannon, Lightning |

Only Moths need a new model; Undertaker uses the existing rise-from-floor animation.

## Night by night
| Night | You learn | Old enemies | New | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Q notes, parry, walls, maze | Bonehead, Legger, Knucklebones, Big Lad | none | The Night Manager (field) |
| 2 | E notes | + Hush, Spanner | Door-to-Door | none; heavy last push |
| 3 | Elites and stagger | + Pot-shot | Last Orders, Busker | The Foreman (drum speeds its escort) |
| 4 | Your maze is under attack | + Crossing Guard | Digger, Undertaker, Moths | The Night Manager + Undertaker escort |
| 5 | Everything together | all | none | The Landlord (stage boss) |

The Landlord: walks in at the top of the song and fights through every section. Stomp (2-bar telegraph, cracks nearby walls) and Volley (4-8 bolt strings at the camp, new string each chorus); staggers like a field boss; calls a Last Orders in the last chorus. The Peeker waits for a later campaign.
