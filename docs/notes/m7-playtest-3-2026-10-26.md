# M7 playtest, round 3 (Jared, 2026-10-26)

Notes as they come in, on the round 3 build (D-0041). Design points wait for the full pass (rule 6); plain bugs are fixed as found.

1. **Boss parry (option A) first impression**: "not sure I like the new parry"; took a minute to realise the parry is a timer on the boss and the orbs heading for the Conductor have no window. On the boss it is cluttered: three notes around it, disabled towers, the parry ring and the counter. (Design; discuss after the pass.)
2. **Towers get notes while locked and still do damage.** Not intended. Two causes: the boss quiet chart picked the nearest tower without checking locks (new in round 3), and a note assigned two beats early stayed on a tower that was locked after (old). Fixed: a locked tower's ring moves to a ready tower; with every tower locked the note is dropped without a miss; the boss chart and elite staggers only pick ready towers. Pushed.
3. **Clutter on the left**: an efficient maze routes creeps around a few strong towers, so every indicator (three note rings, locked towers, the parry ring, the counter flash) lands in one spot and is hard to parse. Jared wonders whether the 3D view with more space per tower fixes it; he pictures the indicator flashing above the tower (reference image: a low-poly wooden watchtower with a Q ring above it). Not changing it in the flat greybox.
4. **Parry on the throw (option A) is pulled**: "parry needs to be on when the attack lands. So we should go back." Reverted the same day (bolts parried as they land at the Conductor again).
5. **Final run**: lost on wave 15 in 6:17; notes 160/262 (61%), Perfects 97; parries 26/69; counters 0/19; towers locked 30, shields lost 14; boss 41 bolts, 1 break. Per wave: W5 notes 8/19, parries 1/16, hp -16 (the wave 5 boss took almost all the health); W10 parries 4/12, hp -2; W11 to W14 notes about 60%, parries 5/8, counters 0/5; W15 fell at 1 break. "I didn't play very well, the parries threw me off significantly."
6. **Jared's call: move on.** Core loop liked; parries and counters may change with the graphics change and distance ("the flat nature makes this stuff feel too close"); balance after. Goal: a private demo for a few friends and colleagues, the 20 levels with graphics and music, synthy note sounds (Perfect, Miss) by then. Asked for the recommended step and open questions; "this is my first game and I need guidance, if I'm rushing let me know."
