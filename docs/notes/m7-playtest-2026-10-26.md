# M7 playtest, round 1 (Jared, 2026-10-26)

Jared's notes as sent, one full 20-wave run (lost on the wave 20 boss at 13:06, level 18, 17 talents, Cannon/Frost/Poison/Light rung 2, Lightning rung 1, 14 walls, counters 34 of 88). Collected here before any change (rule 6). Thread replies and decisions reference the numbered points.

## Bugs (fix without discussion)

1. **UI unreadable.** Help lines overlap the tower panel and the draft; the seventh build button spills off the right edge on Jared's screen, so Frost could not be reached by button.
2. **Fork buttons do nothing.** Poison at rung 2 shows Poison-splash / Virulent; clicking either with enough gold does nothing. Jared never got a fork all run ("hit a power wall because of it").
3. **Pause.** Pauses some mechanics but not the enemies. After clicking Pause with the mouse the button keeps keyboard focus, so Space toggles pause instead of parrying: a loop of missing notes, pausing, parrying. Almost lost the run on wave 19.

## Feel and design (discuss, then agree)

4. **Wave 5 boss:** "felt great"; counter-attack a nice touch; went Rhythm and Defence to see how it changes the rhythm.
5. **Cards:** did not notice a draft before wave 10 (there was one after wave 5, shown in his screenshot). Drafts feel very rare; investing in luck to get one more grey at wave 15 makes progression feel slow. Options he names: every 2 or 3 levels, or on another tell, e.g. mini bosses between big bosses. Overall: progression did not stand out; needs more cards and more stat increases for luck and XP to make sense.
6. **Boss waves:** the escorts die and then the boss stands alone for too long. Wants enemies through most of the boss phase (about 70% of it).
7. **Attack variety:** every stagger gives the same Q E Q E rhythm, predictable. The two or three parry types are good with the counter. Counter-attack feels very strong on bosses (stagger damage) and weak on small creeps. Open question from Jared: should elites stagger too, or should counter stagger on bosses be nerfed?
8. **Upgrades invisible:** without tower visuals, percentage increases feel marginal next to notes and parries; "you really just rely on stagger". A balance discussion point.
9. **Counter and note on the same beat:** often both land on one beat and you must choose; if intended it may be too hard, not satisfying.
10. **Power wall:** towers the same strength for many waves (minus talents); gold went into a bigger maze. (Partly bug 2.)
11. **Talents:** likes them all; some feel redundant and unexciting. Consolidate repeats into WoW-style multi-rank nodes (0/2, 0/3), gate deeper rows on points spent, and give the tree true paths: start in Defence, cross over after counter-attack toward another area's capstone (PoE shape, WoW size and depth). More capstones and unique nodes.
12. **Towers feel the same** apart from slows and stops; poison not noticed. May be a UX, SFX or asset problem.
13. **Wave 20:** lost, "a true challenge", not sure it is overpowered since he was unprepared. "We hit a sweet spot": fix the issues before looking at balance again, as talents and upgrades will change things.
