## M6a playtest, feedback collected (2026-10-25, not yet acted on)

Jared, full run, wave 10 cleared at 2 health (Q 84%, E 72%; 15 pulses, 4 arrows, 15 parried, 6 locks, 3 shields lost; boss 12 bolts, 6 parries, 2 hits, 1 break; 20/20 walls, 84 gold unspent at the end).

- Wave 4: the first Archer died before it shot. Archers in general need to fire from further than 10 tiles out; he barely saw attacks until the end. Arrows should not follow the maze: a straight arrow over the maze. The boss keeps its energy-ball-along-the-path attack. Two distinct attack types.
- Wave 5: boss "okay", missed a few parries and still smoked it; wave 5 could ramp a bit. Balance later.
- Overall fun; the ramp to wave 10 is about right if real games are longer.
- Wants a streak tracker: a small Guitar Hero style "on fire" indicator (a little ball), maybe with a tiny gold multiplier (1-3%, nothing that breaks the economy) or some other small bonus. Nothing decided except that visible streak feedback is needed, subtle, paired with the music.
- Music still awkward: the note-hit and parry sounds feel out of place. Wants custom electronic synth or bass sounds that are half FX, half musical, for attack, block and parry.
- "Lots of attack and enemy variation is going to be key."

## M6c built (2026-10-25, D-0036)

The clickable layer (`game/scripts/hud.gd`): build bar, tower panel with Upgrade and Sell, draft cards and run screens as buttons, P pause, 26x16 landscape board. Keys unchanged.

Headless and xvfb bots:

- Click on a tower selects it and opens the panel with the right title; Upgrade raises the tier and the button greys to "Upgraded"; a build button selects that build; the wave button starts the wave; a card button picks and closes the draft; the end box shows the run lines on a win.
- Pause: song time held exactly across 24 frames (0.488 s before and after). Found and fixed on the way: `AudioStreamPlayer.stream_paused` is not kept when the player has no live playback, so BeatClock now holds the paused time itself and re-syncs on unpause.
- Layout fixed from screenshots: the bar was positioned off screen (a `ready` signal connected after it had fired) and the panel sat on top of the help lines; now the bar and panel are laid out every frame from the window size, the help lines hide while a panel is open, and the drawn "N for endless" line is gone in M6.
- Regression bots for M3, M4 and M5 pass on the wider board.

Screenshots: project `screens/rework/m6c-panel.png`, `m6c-draft.png`, `m6c-end.png`.
