## Carryover: what persists, what resets

I recommend this rule: **what you earn about yourself carries over, what you build on the map stays behind.** Each camp is a fresh map (D-0048), so the board and its gold reset. Your skill, your picks and your tower roster grow.

| Thing | Carries over? | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talent points and XP level | Yes | Spent between nights (D-0051). Aim for 3 or 4 points a night, not the 9 or 10 you get now. |
| Cards | Yes | One pick after each night: 4 or 5 picks in the demo. |
| Tower roster (unlocked types) | Yes | Unlocked through talents (your Arsenal branch), so they carry over with the talents. |
| Type upgrades (gold, rung 0 to 5) | Open | Goes to the upgrade review. In a 2 to 3 minute night you barely get one upgrade and clicks are precious, so likely fewer, bigger upgrades. Making them persist is the alternative, but then an early upgrade pays twice (night and campaign). |
| Gold | No | Each level file sets its start gold. Leftover gold turns into a little XP, so saving isn't wasted. |
| Towers and walls on the map | No | Left behind at the old camp (the story already says this). |
| Health, shields, restores | Health: yes | Health lasts the whole campaign (your call). Shields and restores still reset each night unless the talent review says otherwise. |
| Losing a night | Ends the run | It's a rogue-lite. For testing, the demo also offers "retry this night" with what you had when it started. |

Decided: health carries across the five nights, like Slay the Spire, and we tune the nights knowing that. Talents like Melodic Healing become the way back from a bad night.

## Tower unlocks across the campaign

Decided: towers are unlocked only through the talent tree (your Arsenal branch). The unlocks you earn during a night (Poison from 3 Frost towers, Lightning from 10 Perfects, Light from a Shade kill) are removed from the campaign, and there is no separate tower pick between nights.

- **Night 1** starts with Wall, Cannon, Splash and Frost.

How many towers the tree unlocks, and what they cost in points, is set in the talent review. The in-night unlocks come out of the game when the build phase starts, so frozen Night 1 isn't touched before then. Multiplayer decides its own rule later.

## Locking the scope

The scope locks when you have marked up this doc, the progression budget and the UI slice. From then on, a new mechanic goes to the backlog, not into the demo.

**In the demo:** five nights, seven towers, Q and E notes, parry, stagger, counter, two bosses, cards, talents, towers unlocked through talents, the eyeline UI (slice A plus what the playtest asks for), and the current art kit.

**Out of the demo (backlog):** endless, multiplayer, a tower balance pass beyond what the five nights need, new art style and custom models, the sound pass beyond the synth notes you want for the private demo, community songs, and the wall juggling rule.

**Balancing comes last:** once all five nights are built, the bots run each night at three skill levels, then you play the whole run. The five nights are tuned as a curve, not one at a time.
