# M6a follow-up package (proposal, 2026-10-25)

From Jared's M6a playtest (`prototypes/M6-results.md`). Small build, one playtest.

1. **Archers shoot early and straight.** An Archer shoots as soon as it is within 16 tiles of the Conductor as the crow flies (not path tiles), then every 4 bars. The arrow flies a straight line over the maze to the Conductor, landing on the next downbeat; the Conductor ring and pips stay. Boss bolts keep the energy ball along the path. Two reads: arrow = straight shot, ball = rolling down your maze.
2. **Streak ball.** A small ball next to the Conductor's shields that fills with the streak and changes colour per intensity step (grey, white, yellow, gold-with-glow), with a little pulse on each hit and a drop on a miss. Numbers stay in the HUD line. No gold bonus yet: the economy is in a good spot, and a bonus is a better draft pick in M6b than a passive. If a bonus is wanted now, +1% kill gold per intensity step is the safe version.
3. **Musical hit and parry sounds.** Replace the bell chimes with short synth voices in E (the bed's key): note Good = a plucked pad on E3, Perfect = E4 with a bright transient, parry = a filtered bass stab E2, perfect parry = stab plus a high click, lock = a low detuned thud, miss = a muted tick. All under 300 ms so they sit on the grid.
4. **Wave 5 ramp.** Not now; noted in `tasks` under T-019.

Open from Jared's notes, for the M6b design: attack and enemy variety as a first-class goal.
