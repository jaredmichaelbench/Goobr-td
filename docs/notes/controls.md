# Controls: baseline v0.1

_Started 2026-10-23 from Jared's comment on boss-design open question 1. This replaces the earlier one-key "Conduct" baseline (see D-0008)._

## Jared's original vision

- Traditional keyboard play: **1234 / QWERT**-style binds.
- **Not MMO-like.** Closer to **League of Legends**: 4 primary binds, plus a few for special abilities.
- **B** (and similar) for build.
- **Mouse** for pinging and navigating the map.
- **Everything rebindable.**

## Proposed default layout (all rebindable)

| Key | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Q** | Instrument 1 (e.g. Kick towers) | Fires that instrument's Resonance on its accent |
| **W** | Instrument 2 (e.g. Hat towers) | |
| **E** | Instrument 3 (e.g. Bass towers) | |
| **R** | Ultimate: Crescendo / Overcharge | Long cooldown or a meter built from clean play. Big moment. |
| **Space** | Parry | The souls key. Boss and elite attacks aimed at the Conductor. |
| **F** | Restore | Limited charges, timed on the downbeat after suppression. |
| **1–4** | Items and consumables | Panic buttons, shop items (later). |
| **B** | Build mode | Then hotkeys pick the tower or wall, and the mouse places. In the demo these are 1-4 (D-0012). |
| **Mouse** | Camera, pings, placement in build mode, inspect | No mouse aiming during waves. |

QWER being instruments is the big change from the earlier baseline. Accents are **colored by instrument**, so a kick accent wants Q and a bass accent wants E. A crossfire where two instruments overlap could take a two-key chord (e.g. Q+E) for a bigger hit, which is the "playing your maze" fantasy.

## Why this still isn't Guitar Hero

- Keys are pressed on **accents your maze creates**, not on a scrolling chart. A bad maze gives you few accents, and a great maze gives you many.
- QWER work like League abilities: punctuation with intent, not every beat.
- Missing an accent costs nothing. The towers still auto-fire.

## Open questions

1. Are Q/W/E bound to **tower types** (instruments) or to **map zones/groups** the player assigns? Types are simpler; zones scale better when there are more than 3 tower types.
2. What happens when there are more tower types than keys? Options: you bring a loadout of 3 instruments per run (League champion style, and it fits roguelite drafting), or towers share keys by family.
3. Is R a cooldown, a meter, or both?
4. Is Parry on Space right, or does it belong on a QWER-adjacent key like D? Needs a feel test.
5. Do chords (two keys at once on mixed crossfire) add fun or fatigue?
6. Controller support later via Steam Input: QWER maps onto face buttons, parry onto a shoulder button.
