Jared's notes as sent, one full 20-wave run on the round 2 build (won at 8/20 health in 15:09, level 19, 17 talents in Rhythm and Defence with Counter-attack, Cannon 5 Siege, Frost 3 Brittle, Lightning 3 Fork, Poison 2, seven picks). Collected before any change (rule 6).

The run's numbers from his end screen: notes 350 of 648 (54%), Perfects 221; parries 146 of 218 (67%); counters 44 of 97 (45%); towers locked 54, shields lost 12, Restores 3; boss 116 bolts, 10 stomps, 17 breaks; crits 89, arcs 2480, poison 22116; stats damage +95%, luck +7.

## Bugs and UI

1. **Text still spills onto the map** at wave 0 ("still a UI disaster, surprised we couldn't get this resolved in 2 attempts"). His screenshot: the status and help lines run under the board's left edge.
2. **Fork buttons greyed** (Fork / Static on Lightning at rung 2) and not clickable; the U and I keys worked. Screenshot shows both fork buttons dim while Sell is bright.
3. **Card history**: a card came up after the wave 5 boss ("increases cards received", grey) and he could not re-read it. Wants a history of picks.

## Feel and design

4. **Waves 1 to 3 too easy**: time to kill is very short at rungs 0 to 3, no challenge. Rebalance (health or damage, unsure).
5. **Wave 5 boss** fairly easy; could take more enemies. The grey card he picked felt "incredibly powerful for a grey".
6. **Wave 12**: hard to kill, missing many more notes and parries, especially double parries. Before, a quick tap after a parry landed the counter; now it has a delay. More E in the mix is good but throws the rhythm; "my stats feel awful", needs a closer look.
7. **Shades** (veiled, need a landed note to crack): incredibly hard, run across the map while you wait for notes. Too Bloons-like. Proposal from Jared: take reduced damage while the veil holds (90/80/70%), notes damage the veil, so you are not just waiting for notes.
8. **Wave 13, parry and counter**: both a bit too hard to hit. The counter ring should match the note ring's size and style for timing. Notes, parries and counters still overlap. Wants them separate and in a flow: **Q is the primary key, E the accent**; for the first 15 waves E follows Q in predictable ways; Crescendos, late waves and late bosses flip it to throw the player. Break from the pattern only after the player is comfortable.
9. **Wave 15 boss**: after the first pack it is boring; the trickle packs are too small next to the first pack, "you know it's just a parry game". Wants notes between the parries on bosses (none right now while you wait for the attack). **Eye split**: notes on the left (towers), attacks landing on the right (Conductor), counter timing on the boss; darting back and forth is hard. Considers a layout with the maze end in the centre, or another solution.
10. **Wave 20**: beat it; given the frustration and an easy boss, tuning is needed. The counter in this state is not worth taking, frustrating and hard to land (did not spec into notes, a wider window might have helped). Asks for backend stats.
