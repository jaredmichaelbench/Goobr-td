## Cards (proposal)
- 4 cards a run (pick of 3 after nights 1-4). Talents are the plan, cards are the twist; no card repeats a talent.
- No flat stats; one-line text; draft-only cards (reroll, wide hand, luck) removed.
- Rarity: Rare ~65%, Epic ~28%, Legendary ~7% (max one a run); luck nudges up.
- Starter 20: Chain snipe, Undertow, Lighthouse (new), Shatter, Piercing shot, Siege engine (new), Sharpshooter, Rally, Echo, Off-beat (new), Heavy boots, Light boots, Wanted posters, Dread, Blood money (new: -3 health, +100 gold each night), Difficulty tome, Glass cannon, Fermata (L), Haunting (L), Joe's dagger (L).
