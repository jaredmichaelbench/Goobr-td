# M6a proposal: TD depth plus enemy variety (2026-10-25)

Jared's polish-pass feedback (`prototypes/M5-results.md`) adds enemy variety, boss escorts, a music bed and small parry tweaks to the agreed M6a (third tower, upgrades, economy). Proposal to discuss.

## Quick fixes (no debate needed, say if you disagree)

1. **Parry window for elites and boss: Good 90 → 110 ms.** Perfect stays 35. Note windows unchanged.
2. **Parry return comes from the tower.** When you parry a pulse, the attacked tower fires back at the elite (its own bolt, its own damage x3), not a shot from the Conductor. Makes sense and makes the tower choice matter.
3. **Unspent gold nudge.** The "Press N" line shows "Gold unspent: 80" in yellow when you can afford something.
4. **Boss escorts.** The boss wave spawns a small creep pack with it and another about 10 s later (a third of a normal wave each).

## Two elite types (enemy variety, discuss)

Both read the same way: a projectile leaves the elite, you watch it land, Space on arrival.

- **Jammer** (today's elite): walks up to a tower and throws a pulse at it; unparried, the tower locks. Short range is the point: it is the "get it before it reaches you" enemy. Readability fix: it stops walking for the bar it winds up (stands still, sword up), so the throw is never from on top of the tower, and the projectile has a longer visible path. Parry = the tower fires back.
- **Archer** (new): stops at range on the path and shoots the Conductor. Long flight along the path, same read as the boss bolt. Parry = the Conductor deflects it back for damage. Unparried = one Conductor shield gone; with no shields left, 1 health. Shields: 3 per wave, shown as pips at the goal. Creeps walking in still cost health, shields don't help.

Waves mix them: wave 3 one Jammer, wave 4 one Archer, wave 5 boss plus escorts, from wave 6 both.

Why both rather than replacing the lock: the lock is what makes Restore matter, and the Archer gives the long-telegraph parry Jared asked for without changing the balance of the Jammer.

## Music bed (quick version)

Replace the synth stems with a bass-and-rhythm bed: a sub-bass pulse on the beat and a soft kick, no hats, no lead. With a real song playing, the bed sits under it at low volume and only the song ducks on a miss, so a miss sounds plain, not bad. Intensity: the song's volume and a high-shelf open up with the note streak (every 4 hits in a row opens one step; a miss drops one). Full treatment stays in the Music later-area.

## Still in M6a as agreed

Third tower (slow; its note is a freeze), one upgrade tier per tower, economy pass (T-019).

## Order

Quick fixes and the music bed first (one build), then the two elite types, then third tower and upgrades, each with a playtest. Boss escorts ride with the elite build.
