## Jared's notes (verbatim, lightly grouped)

### Levels 1-5

- All levels are too easy in their current state: more health, less tower damage, something. The early levels also need to last longer to give a better sense of what is happening. Maybe health solves both, but the difficulty and the time spent clearing are way too low.
- The boss died after 2 notes into the crescendo, again too fast.
- Talents go unspent constantly. We don't reward the player for leveling up, and the XP bar is barely noticeable. I pool them up, then remember. The Talents button turns green, but maybe it should shake like "HEY OVER HERE". Still undecided.
- Menus and UI look much better, but we can do better. The Talents, Restore and Start wave buttons don't belong with the towers; that should be your build area.
- Proposal: icons for Talents, Start and Restore in the centre middle of the bottom panel. Rounded buttons with actions, hotkeys and tooltips on hover. Refresh pulse sits in the middle, with Talents and Start wave left or right. These are game controls, not build options. "My preference anyways, we can discuss after the play test is finished."
- The XP bar should move to the bottom, under those buttons, and look like a World of Warcraft XP bar with bubbles and percents.
- Rogue-lite cards still don't appear enough, and they aren't dialed in. We need to review all of them again with another lens; they feel boring. Their UI is stale and they lack information.
- Talent tooltips and descriptions are weak. A multi-rank talent should show its running total (e.g. "next rank grants 8% for a total of 16%"); right now it just says 8% per rank.
- The talents menu pauses the music, which is jarring. Fix it.
- Jared will send sketches of the talent tree he wants to try and of how the cards should look.
- Parry and notes now feel much better (early levels); more feedback to come.

### Level 10

- The boss and enemies are still far too easy with all the power-ups we've added.
- Notes can fall on the same tower in succession. A Q and an E land on one tower, and while the Q counts down the E starts, which is confusing. Always use separate towers; note hits have infinite range, so placement shouldn't affect gameplay.
- Talent tooltips need a review (see the tooltip image: "Resonance (shared: Rhythm or Towers) crossfire +20% per tower"). What does it mean: 20% damage, 20% crossfire? What is crossfire? The text all bleeds together. A modern tooltip shows plain text in white, effects and known concepts in blue, upgrades in green, and so on. It needs a clear title, clear explainer text, and clear values with what they affect.
- Talent tooltips appear after a delayed hover, which is very annoying. They need to be more reactive.
- The talent linking isn't what he imagined; the sketch will cover it.
- Not a fan of either beat-tracking feature: the 8 boxes in the selected-tower pane, or the bar up top. They don't serve a purpose; we are parrying and landing attacks. The Guitar Hero timeline should go. We need to improve our UX so users don't ask for something like that.
- Still lost on what keeps a tower locked (Poison etc.). Move these locks into the talent tree instead of the catch-all bonus on today's last capstone: the Towers tree unlocks tower types with point investment. More in the write-up. Poison being locked now feels awkward because nothing explains how to unlock it.
- The Light tower's tooltips are deceiving or poorly explained. The upgrade gives +40% damage, but to what? Towers in its range, or its own base damage (which is zero)?

### Level 15

- Still too easy, on health and scaling.
- Notes come way too fast and don't feel tied to the music. He hit 20 of 55. Go back to the right tempo and cadence: a sequence, nail it, pause, the next sequence comes up, nail it. Early sequences are just Q then E. By the end they keep the same slower tempo but become non-obvious, so the player has to think, without speeding up a ton (see video).
- He played on to wave 20. Bosses feel good: a stagger means a longer rhythm sequence. The later levels feel like note spam, over and over. Bring the boss feeling, shorter, to regular levels too: less often, more bursty, more rhythm.
